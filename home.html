The Simpsons is an American animated sitcom, created by Matt Groening, that follows the daily misadventures of Homer, Marge, Bart, Lisa, and Maggie. It is a satirical depiction of American life, and is set in the fictional town of Springfield.
After the show's debut in 1989 there has been a total of 37 seasons, which have earner the series the title of the greatest television series of the 20th century.
