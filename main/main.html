The Simpsons is an American animet series created by Matt Groening. It is a satirical depiction of American life, set in the fictional town of Springfield, in an inspecified location in the United States of America.

Since it's debut in 1989, 805 episodes of the show have been broadcasted and it has been named the greatest television series of the 20th century. And it became Fox's first series to land in the Top 30 ratings in a season (1989-1990).
