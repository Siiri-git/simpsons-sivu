Apu Nahasapeemapetilon

Apu is the proprietor of the Kwik-E-Mart, a popular convenience store in Springfield, and is known for his catchphrase, "Thank you, come again."
Apu is an Indian immigrant who graduated first in his class at Calcutta Technical Institute (Caltech) and holds a Ph.D. in computer science.
