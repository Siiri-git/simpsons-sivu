The Simpsons Page

Springfield Characters Forum

The Simpson, created in 1989, is an American animates sitcom created by Matt Groening. The main characters are a satire of a working-class family, consisting of Homer, Marge, Bart, Lisa, and Maggie.
It was names the greatest television series of the 20th century, and is widely regarder as one of the most influental animeted series of all time. It was also Fox's first series to land in the Top 30 ratings in a season (1989–1990).
