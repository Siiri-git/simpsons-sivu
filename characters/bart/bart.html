Bartholomew Jo-Jo "Bart" Simpson

Bart is the mischievous and rebellious eldest child of the Simpson family. He is known for his mischief, rebelliousness, and disrespect for authority.
Bart often engages in pranks and is characterized as a troublemaker at Springfield Elementary School.
Some of his famous catchphrases are "Eat my shorts!", "¡Ay, caramba!", and "Don't have a cow, man!"
